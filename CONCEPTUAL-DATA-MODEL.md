# CONCEPTUAL-DATA-MODEL

## Purpose
This document defines the business concepts and relationships for the SensorApp modernization. It is technology-independent and provides the foundation for the Logical and Physical Data Models.
---

## Modeling Principles
- Business capability driven
- Technology independent
- Single source of truth
- Clear ownership boundaries
- Consistent business terminology
---

---

## Core Business Concepts

| Business Concept | Description                                         |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Device           | Physical sensor registered in the platform.         |
| Configuration    | Operational settings assigned to a device.          |
| Sensor Reading   | Telemetry generated by a device.                    |
| Alert            | Business event raised when thresholds are exceeded. |
| Audit Event      | Immutable operational record for traceability.      |
 
---

## Business Relationships
- Device owns one or more Configurations.
- Device generates many Sensor Readings.
- Sensor Reading may trigger an Alert.
- Alert creates Audit Events.
---

## Business Lifecycle

```text
Register Device
      |
Configure Device
      |
Receive Telemetry
      |
Evaluate Rules
      |
Generate Alert
      |
Write Audit Event
```
---

## Assumptions
- Devices have unique identities.
- Configurations are managed independently.
- Telemetry grows significantly faster than master data.
- Audit events are append-only.
---

## Transition
The Logical Data Model adds entities, attributes, keys, and relationships while preserving the business concepts defined here.
---

## Conclusion
This conceptual model establishes the common business vocabulary for the proposed architecture and provides a stable foundation for logical and physical implementation.
